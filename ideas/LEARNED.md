# What the factory learned

When a backlog idea becomes a scene, compare the critic's scores and note with how the scene actually
turned out, and write a line here about what the scoring missed. Newest at the bottom.

## Batch 2026-10-29-1 (4 hands, 12 pitches)

- Each trio of pitches from one hand converged on the same mechanic (three eclipse spirals, three
  "rival lantern" pairs). The critic's `distinct` score caught it. Worth trying fewer pitches per hand
  and more hands.
- The critic's best fixes were about the camera: move it to where canvas is strong (frontal, flat,
  silhouette) and away from where it's weak (curved perspective, funnels, 3D coils).
